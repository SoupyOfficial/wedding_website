### How to Use This Form

1. Open the live website at the URL above.
2. For each item, test it on both desktop and mobile.
3. Check off (`✓`) items that work correctly. Mark `✗` for issues.
4. Rate each section's overall experience from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
5. Use the blank lines to describe any issues in detail.
6. Think like each of these guests while you review:

> **Persona 1 — Grandma Jean (non-technical older guest):** Not comfortable with technology. Needs large, clear text. Gets confused by too many options or jargon. Expects obvious buttons and simple navigation. Wants clear directions.

> **Persona 2 — Cousin Tyler (tech-savvy younger guest):** Lives on his phone. Expects smooth animations, modern design, shareable content. Judges loading speed harshly. Will try edge cases.

> **Persona 3 — Guest on a phone at the airport:** Spotty connection. Small screen. One-handed scrolling. Needs forms that are easy to fill on mobile.

> **Persona 4 — Aunt Sarah (vision-impaired):** Needs high contrast, readable fonts, alt text on images. Uses screen reader or zoom. Relies on clear focus indicators.

> **Persona 5 — Uncle Mark (dietary restrictions):** Needs a clear way to communicate dietary restrictions and allergies on the RSVP form.
